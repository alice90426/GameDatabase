# Game Information

## Introduction
- Size: 6 reels, 566665 rows.
- Lines: 32400 Ways.
- Features: Cascade Reel, Symbol Transform, Multiplier Symbol, Win Multiplier, Free Game.
---

## Symbols
|  Symbol   | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| [H1]~[H6] | High Pay    |
| [L1]~[L5] | Low Pay     |
| [WD]      | Wild        |
| [SC]      | Scatter     |

### Wild Symbol
- [WD] substitutes for all symbols except [SC].
- [WD] appears on reels 2, 3, 4 or 5.

### Scatter Symbol
- 4 [SC] anywhere on the reels trigger 10 free spins. Each extra 1 [SC] awards 2 free spin.
---

## Game Features

### Cascade Reel
- All winning symbol will be eliminated after scoring, a replacement will fall from the remaining symbols above, replacement symbols may continue to score until there are no winning combinations.

### Symbol Transform
- From reels 2 to 5, some symbols with the blue frames.
- If the winning symbols have the blue frames, the blue frames will be upgrated into yellow frames.
- If the winning symbols have the yellow frames, the symbols in that frames will be upgrated into full reel [WD].

### Symbol Transform
- Each time an [H1] appears, the multiplier increases by X2 until the end of the round.

### Free Game
- 4 [SC] anywhere on the reels retrigger 10 free spins. Each extra 1 [SC] awards 2 free spin.
- The initial multiplier is X2. Each time an [H1] appears, the multiplier increases by X2, until the free spins end.
